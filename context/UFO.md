## The 6% Transaction Fee — How It Works
Every buy, sell, and transfer charges a 6% fee immediately distributed as follows:
- 1% reflected to holders
- 1% burns UFO (sent to dead address)
- 1% burns pTGC (sent to dead address)
- 3% paid to LP providers

## LP Providers — The UFO Shares System
UFO's most unique feature is its never-before-done LP shares technology:
- LP providers earn 1 UFO share for every 1 UFO input into LP

- Shares determine your position in the reward pool for the 3% LP fee
